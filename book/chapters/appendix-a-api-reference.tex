\chapter{API Quick Reference}
\label{ch:api-reference}

This appendix provides lookup tables for the most commonly used Kiss APIs.  For full documentation, see the JavaDoc (backend) and JSDoc (frontend).

\section{Connection}
\label{sec:api-connection}

\begin{longtable}{P{4.8cm}P{5.7cm}}
\toprule
\textbf{Method} & \textbf{Description} \\
\midrule
\endhead
\texttt{execute(sql, args...)} & Execute INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE. \\
\texttt{fetchOne(sql, args...)} & Fetch single Record (or null). \\
\texttt{fetchAll(sql, args...)} & Fetch all Records as List. \\
\texttt{fetchAllJSON(sql, args...)} & Fetch as JSONArray. \\
\texttt{query(sql, args...)} & Return Cursor for iteration. \\
\texttt{exists(sql, args...)} & True if any records match. \\
\texttt{newRecord(table)} & Create Record for insertion. \\
\texttt{newCommand()} & Create Command for fine control. \\
\texttt{newQueryBuilder()} & Create QueryBuilder with auto-joins. \\
\texttt{commit()} & Commit current transaction. \\
\texttt{rollback()} & Rollback current transaction. \\
\texttt{getSchemaGraph()} & Get or build the SchemaGraph. \\
\texttt{getDBType()} & Get the database type. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\section{SortBuilder}
\label{sec:api-sortbuilder}

\begin{longtable}{P{4.8cm}P{5.7cm}}
\toprule
\textbf{Method} & \textbf{Description} \\
\midrule
\endhead
\texttt{key(key, exprs...)} & Register a sort key with one or more expressions; returns this. \\
\texttt{join(joinSql, keys...)} & Join emitted once when any listed key is used; returns this. \\
\texttt{text(col)} & Static. Case-insensitive text; empty/whitespace is blank. \\
\texttt{zeroBlank(col)} & Static. Number or int date; 0 is blank. \\
\texttt{raw(sql)} & Static. Verbatim SQL; must yield NULL when blank. \\
\texttt{textSql(type, col)} & Static. The \texttt{text()} SQL for a \texttt{ConnectionType}. \\
\texttt{build(type, sorts, default, tiebreak...)} & Return a \texttt{Result}. Blanks last; unknown keys rejected. \\
\texttt{Sort.of(key, asc)} & A requested sort. \\
\texttt{Result.orderBy()} & \texttt{order by ...} clause, or empty. \\
\texttt{Result.joins()} & Needed joins, each once, trailing space. \\
\texttt{Result.joinList()} & Needed joins as a list. \\
\texttt{Result.rejectedKeys()} & Unknown non-blank keys (client text). \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
